About Us

The barbers here at Walnut Street Barber Shop have proudly provided the men of Springfield and the surrounding area with professional, excellent service since 1943. Experience matters — every barber on our team has years behind the chair, understands the cuts you want, and knows how to deliver exactly what you ask for.

The Springfield community has recognized that excellence over the years. We’ve been voted “Best Barber Shop” by readers of the Springfield News-Leader, featured on the cover of 417 Magazine and voted Best of 417 numerous times, and named “Best Barber Shop” year after year by readers of the Missouri State University Standard. Fair, reasonable pricing matters too — it always has.
