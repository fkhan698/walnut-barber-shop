What We Offer

Services

Haircuts

Buzzcut

Hair trimmed to 3/8 inch or less all over with clippers, includes tapering around neck and ears. Does not include fades, high-and-tights, “leave the top alone” cuts, undercuts or flat tops.

Regular Cut

Clipper cut on sides and back. Clipper or shear cutting of the top. Top lengths of 4 inches or less.

Fade

Standard, low, medium, or high zero fades. Top hair lengths of 4 inches or less. Does not include Mohawk or Faux Hawk fades.

Specialty Cut

Top lengths over 4 inches, side and back lengths over 1 inch, Mohawks, Faux Hawks, specialty mullets & drastic fades. Any combination of clipper and/or shear cutting used at the barber’s discretion.

Beard Trims

Short Beard / Goatee

Freshening up of the jawline, neckline and overall shape of beard, goatee and mustache. Maintains an already established shape. Overall length no greater than 3/8 inch.

Long Beard / Goatee

Shaping of long beard, goatee and mustache. Establishing a jawline, neckline and mustache, with parts greater than 3/8 inch. Specialty shaping.
